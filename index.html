The Avengers watchlist

The Avengers are a team of superheroes and one of the most iconic superhero teams in the Marvel
Cinematic Universe (MCU). Based on the famous Marvel Comics team created by Stan Lee and Jack Kirby,
the Avengers were brought together to protect Earth from powerful threats that no single hero
could face alone. The team was formed under the leadership of S.H.I.E.L.D. director Nick Fury,
who believed that Earth's greatest heroes could work together to defend the world.

The Avengers are made up of powerful and gifted individuals, each with their own abilities,
personalities, backgrounds, and stories. From Iron Man and Captain America to Thor, Hulk, Black Widow,
and Hawkeye, the original members became the foundation of the team. As the MCU
continued, many other heroes joined the Avengers and fought alongside them in some of the
biggest battles in the Marvel universe.

Throughout their journey, the Avengers have faced dangerous enemies, protected innocent people,
and dealt with conflicts both inside and outside the team. Their story spans several movies and connects
many different characters and events across the MCU. This watchlist is created to keep track
of the Avengers-related movies, including the movies that have been watched and those that are still
waiting to be watched.
